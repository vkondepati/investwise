## Core User Segments

### **1. First-Time Investor**
- Profile: Buying first rental property, high education needs
- Key Needs: Education, confidence, affordability validation
- InvestWise Value: Simplified investment analysis, market comparison, risk assessment

### **2. Active Investor**
- Profile: 5-20 properties, actively trading, optimization focused
- Key Needs: Portfolio tracking, optimization opportunities, performance metrics
- InvestWise Value: Portfolio dashboard, cash flow optimization, tax planning

### **3. Institutional/Portfolio Investor**
- Profile: 50+ properties, institutional infrastructure, efficiency focused
- Key Needs: Reporting, data exports, institutional-grade analytics
- InvestWise Value: Enterprise features, API access, custom integrations

### **4. House Flipper**
- Profile: Short-term holds, renovation focus, exit planning critical
- Key Needs: Deal analysis, ARV (after-repair value) calculation, financing options
- InvestWise Value: Flip analyzer, renovation cost estimation, exit planning

### **5. Multi-Asset Investor**
- Profile: Owns rentals + flips + commercial, portfolio diversification
- Key Needs: Consolidated view, cross-asset comparison, rebalancing guidance
- InvestWise Value: Multi-asset support, consolidated reporting, strategic allocation

## CashFlow Module: Core Capabilities

### **Tier 1: Property-Level Analysis**
- Monthly/annual operating expense breakdown
- Rental income projections (market-based + custom)
- Vacancy modeling
- Net cash flow calculation
- Cap rate and cash-on-cash return
- Break-even analysis

### **Tier 2: Portfolio Aggregation**
- Cross-property cash flow summation
- Income vs. expense trends
- Portfolio-level performance metrics
- Comparative property analysis
- Income concentration risk

### **Tier 3: Advanced Forecasting**
- 5-30 year projections
- Rent growth modeling (historical, market, custom)
- Expense inflation assumptions
- Mortgage paydown tracking
- Refinance impact analysis
- Interest rate sensitivity modeling

### **Tier 4: Scenario Modeling**
- "What-if" analyses (vacancy, rent changes, expenses)
- Economic downturn scenarios
- Rate hike impact projections
- Capital improvement ROI
- Property-level vs. portfolio-level scenarios
- Stress testing (recession, market crash)

### **Tier 5: Optimization Recommendations**
- Underperforming property identification
- Rental rate optimization ("market rent: $2,100 vs. your rent: $1,900")
- Expense reduction opportunities
- Capital improvement prioritization
- Portfolio rebalancing guidance

### **Tier 6: Tax Optimization**
- Depreciation tracking and scheduling
- Cost segregation opportunities (building components)
- Passive loss carryforward management
- 1031 exchange tracking and recommendations
- Estimated tax payment guidance
- Tax-deferred reinvestment strategies

## Assumptions & Constraints

### **Assumptions**
- Investors trust AI recommendations if explanations are clear
- Real estate market data APIs are reliable and available
- Cash flow forecasting accuracy improves with market data integration
- Multi-property investors will consolidate on single platform

### **Constraints**
- MLS data licensing complexity
- Real-time data costs (scalable expense)
- Regulatory uncertainty (investment advice disclaimers needed)
- Data privacy compliance (GDPR, CCPA)
- AI hallucination risks (need guardrails)
- Third-party data dependency

### **Risks**
- Market downturn reducing investment activity
- Competitive response from larger platforms
- Data quality issues affecting forecast accuracy
- Regulatory changes on investment advice
- User churn if accuracy doesn't meet expectations
